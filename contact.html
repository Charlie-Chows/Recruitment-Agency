Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about partnering with Stackly.

How quickly can Stackly present pre-vetted candidate shortlists? ▼

Under our standard SLA, we deliver your first shortlist of 3 pre-screened, video-interviewed candidates within 48 hours of intake alignment.

What happens if a placed candidate leaves during their trial period? ▼

Every direct hire placement comes with our 90-Day Free Replacement Guarantee. If a candidate departs or fails to meet benchmark performance, we replace them at zero added fee.

Do you handle global remote hiring and international payroll compliance? ▼

Yes. We offer end-to-end Employer of Record (EOR), international W2/1099 compliance, and background verification across 30+ countries worldwide.

How do candidate confidential stealth searches work? ▼

For executive search and stealth candidates, we enforce strict NDA protocols. Profiles are shared anonymously without identifying details until formal mutual consent is established.

What fee structures do you offer for enterprise hiring? ▼

We provide flexible engagement models tailored to your scope: Success-Based Direct Hire Fee, Retained Executive Search, and Monthly Fractional RPO Advisory terms.
